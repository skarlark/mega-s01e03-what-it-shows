Proposed replacement for subject-only triage

Decision 0001 remains unchanged as accepted history. This record proposes its replacement; the desk must adopt the rule before practice changes.

  1. Read each body and establish the product, intended date, observed date, tenant locale, booking form and channel.
  2. Check tenant settings. Browser locale and intake tags are clues, not reliable cause labels.
  3. Use evidence to choose the incident boundary. Matching subjects alone are insufficient. File separate tickets for different demonstrated mechanisms, even when they cause the same visible symptom.
  4. Preserve unusual symptoms and missing facts for investigation. Do not manufacture a new root cause from a different description. The desk-noted timing reports remain footnoted under their tenant-based cause.
  5. Deduplicate exports by report ID. Each report counts once, even if its ticket or task assignment changes.
  6. Review new reports, update counts and links, and verify every affected path before closing either problem ticket. Re-read stale guidance against current evidence.
