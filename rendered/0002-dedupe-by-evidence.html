The shared parser reads month first even for day-first tenants. Booking-v3 has a different fault: the hint asks for month-first input but the form interprets it using the tenant’s day-first order. Fixing the shared parser alone will not fix that hint.

Five reports explicitly noted by the desk were read as one-day moves or timing anomalies. They are counted under parser by tenant, with their reported behavior preserved as notes on that ticket. Do not create another problem solely from a different symptom, or assert a timezone fix without reproducing it. One report says the invite arrived before booking; inspect creation and delivery timestamps as well as timezone conversion while investigating the parser ticket.

Why the queue looked like one incident

Reports began in April, while merging into the umbrella ticket began in July. The July merge spike reflects accumulated reports being filed, not necessarily a sudden increase in failures. The desk continued merging afterwards. A count of merged reports is not an incidence rate, and neither a queued fix nor a known label proves resolution.

Triage rule from now on

  1. Read the body and establish the product, intended date, observed date, tenant, tenant locale, booking form and channel.
  2. Treat browser locale and intake tags as clues. Check the tenant directory and actual path; the browser may disagree with the tenant.
  3. Merge only when evidence supports the same failure mechanism. Matching subjects are insufficient; different subjects can describe the same fault.
  4. Keep anomalous or unknown reports separate for investigation. Ask for missing facts rather than merging by default.
  5. Deduplicate export rows by report ID. Record a report’s destination and preserve its history when splitting tickets.
  6. Review incoming reports against these causes, update counts and links, and confirm fixes against each affected path before closing either ticket. Re-read stale guidance before relying on it.

Handoff and verification

The public repository contains one open issue for each of the two problems, with report IDs, counts and evidence. For parser, reproduce a day-first date through app/email or booking-v2; preserve the intended day and month in the invite. For hint, reproduce on a day-first booking-v3 form, align the hint with input parsing, and check app/email independently. For the unusual reports within the parser ticket, collect the original invite, stored timestamps, event timezone, recipient timezone, booking creation time and delivery time before choosing a fix.
